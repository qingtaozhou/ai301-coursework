# Rubric: is this reproduction package ready to post?

## Checks

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| claim-specific | Candidate claim comment against the issue body and thread highlights; Comms in the evidence guide. | Identifies the issue's concrete behavior and proposes a bounded investigation, reproduction, or testing contribution. Does not substitute generic enthusiasm or demand a fix, claim exclusive ownership, or promise an unsupported fix. | required |
| environment-recorded | Repro report's environment record against the issue target and repo-facts template asks; Environment in the evidence guide. | Records the tested project version or commit, OS/platform, and relevant runtime, dependency, installation method, configuration, or build profile needed to interpret this issue. States material differences from the reporter's target. Output alone does not establish missing environment facts. | required |
| steps-rerunnable | Repro report's setup, input/configuration, commands or UI actions, and resulting artifacts; Steps in the evidence guide. | A stranger can reach the tested starting state and repeat the trigger using the information posted in the package, without guessing missing input, configuration, fixtures, or consequential actions. Standard tool usage need not be explained; local files required by the trigger must be supplied or described sufficiently to reconstruct a minimal equivalent. Literal bytes are necessary when syntax or exact contents decide the behavior, but ordinary valid dependency lists need not be copied verbatim when the offending section and command are specified. The main evidenced attempt must be repeatable; supplementary attempts may be summarized if the conclusion does not depend on undisclosed details. | required |
| input-faithful | Repro report's literal input, configuration and trigger against the issue's supplied example; Steps and Behavior shown in the evidence guide. | Uses the issue's input and trigger, or explicitly explains changes while preserving the conditions relevant to the reported behavior. A changed input that instead causes an unrelated failure cannot support a reproduction claim. For cannot-reproduce, a relevant attempt with explicitly acknowledged limitations can pass even when the author could not create every suspected failure precondition; do not require a successful recreation of the bug's conditions to report a failed attempt honestly. | required |
| outcome-evidenced | Repro report's output, logs, screenshots or concrete observation record compared with the issue's expected and actual behavior; Behavior shown in the evidence guide. | Evidence establishes what happened when the relevant trigger was tested. A claimed reproduction shows the issue's distinguishing behavior, not merely any error or adjacent failure. An honest cannot-reproduce report passes when a concrete relevant attempt and its results are shown and differences or unachieved preconditions are stated. It need not prove the exact failure preconditions were achieved; it must limit its conclusion to the attempt, without claiming the bug is absent generally. Bare assertions or inaccessible local artifacts are insufficient. | required |
| claims-supported | Claim comment and repro report's conclusions against their posted steps, environment and artifacts; Honesty in the evidence guide. | Reproduction status and expected/actual outcomes agree with the evidence. Observations are separated from hypotheses; claims about cause, platforms, versions, frequency, fixes, or tests do not exceed what is shown. Clearly qualified explanations of a failed attempt are hypotheses, not unsupported certainty. A concrete representative artifact plus a summary of repeated or supplementary runs can suffice; do not demand separate logs for every run or reject based on speculative implementation/ARG_MAX arithmetic that the bundle does not establish. Cannot-reproduce is limited to the tested environment and does not declare the issue invalid. | required |
| repo-conventions | Both candidate comments against the issue thread, repo-facts bug-report asks and contribution/AI-use policy; Comms in the evidence guide. | Supplies the applicable substantive information requested by the repo and respects its contribution rules. These are follow-up reproduction comments on an existing issue: do not require a second title, duplicate search, filing workflow or every diagnostic dump if the posted environment and artifacts already provide the facts needed to assess the reproduction. Missing issue-relevant configuration or environment still fails; explicit AI disclosure requirements must be met as stated. Includes any disclosure required by the stated AI policy, with the required substance; no AI disclosure is invented when no policy requires it. Reports are evaluated as issue comments, not automatically subject to restrictions stated only for PRs. | required |
| useful-comms | Both candidate comments read as posted on the issue thread; Comms in the evidence guide. | Communicates the relevant result and intended next action clearly, without blame, pressure, exaggerated certainty or unrelated boilerplate. A terse report can pass; headings, length, step count and ornamental politeness do not determine readiness. | required |

## Verdict rule

Accept if every applicable required check passes. Reject if any applicable required check fails or is unclear. Preferred checks, if later added, never change the verdict. Missing evidence is unclear; contradictory evidence or an explicitly unmet condition is fail. Never infer missing proof from confidence or formatting.

In a live claim-only draft, apply claim-specific and useful-comms to the claim, and repo-conventions to conventions applicable to the claim. Mark environment-recorded, steps-rerunnable, input-faithful, outcome-evidenced and claims-supported unclear with evidence `not yet applicable: claim-only draft`, and exclude them from the verdict. Still reject a claim that presents unsupported completed-work assertions under claim-specific. In full packages and eval mode, every check applies.
