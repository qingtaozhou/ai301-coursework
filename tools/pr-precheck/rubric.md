# Rubric: is this pull request ready to submit?

## Checks

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plan fidelity | Plan context's scope, approach, exclusions and deviations beside the complete diff and description's implementation claims. | The diff implements the accepted plan within its boundary, or material changes/omissions are explicitly disclosed with what changed and why and remain a bounded response to the issue. Description claims match what the diff actually delivers. Necessary supporting edits need no separate deviation when consistent with the plan. Silent additions, silent omissions, and claims of unchanged behavior contradicted by the diff fail. | required |
| Behavioral evidence | Plan's test plan and original repro beside candidate test evidence and testing claims. | Evidence gives runnable commands or manual steps and observed results for the original failure, with an observable expected-after comparison, plus relevant controls affected by the change. A documented inability or incomplete planned check may pass when the limitation and its impact are explicit and the remaining evidence still demonstrates the changed behavior. Generic “tests pass,” an unexecuted test plan, or an asserted fix without observable proof fails. Automated tests are not mandatory. | required |
| Repository checks | Repo facts/contribution instructions and plan test commands beside captured suite/check results and draft testing section. | The repo's stated applicable checks have actual reported outcomes, including failures. A skipped or blocked check is explicitly identified with a concrete reason and resulting verification limit; do not require a green suite or completion of every planned test. Silence about required checks, unchecked claims of success, or “not run” without a reason fails. Do not invent a suite requirement when the repo states none. | required |
| Reviewable diff | Entire unified diff and commits beside the issue and plan boundary. | A reviewer can isolate the bounded fix and necessary supporting changes without unrelated edits, unexplained formatting churn, debugging leftovers, dead/commented-out implementation blocks, or other debris obscuring it. A legitimate diagnostic or explanatory comment is not debris. Disclosure does not excuse unrelated churn or leftover debug code. Commit count, size alone, and cosmetic polish do not decide. | required |
| Standards and communication | Repo facts' PR-template asks and contribution/AI policy, relevant thread direction, and candidate title/description. | The outgoing text communicates the change and issue accurately and substantively satisfies the repo's stated template, contribution and disclosure requirements and relevant maintainer constraints. N/A with a reason is valid where appropriate. Do not invent a template, disclosure policy, heading, or reply requirement when the bundle states none. Honest uncertainty or disclosed failing checks alone are not a violation. | required |

## Verdict rule

Return accept only if every required check passes. Any required fail or unclear returns reject; unclear means a necessary fact cannot be verified. Preferred checks never affect the verdict. Grade all checks even after a failure. There is no score, majority vote, or accept-with-reservations verdict. Honest limitations may satisfy the relevant pass condition; disclosure cannot repair a false claim, absent behavioral proof, policy violation, or unreviewable diff.
